📋 Important House Rules

  • 🍲 Whoever cooks the food is responsible for washing vessels and cleaning everything used while cooking.
  • 🧽 After cooking, clean the cooktop, kitchen platform, vessels, and sink properly.
  • 🚿 Weekly or every 15 days one person must completely clean the washroom.
  • 🗑️ Use separate dustbins for Dry Waste and Wet Waste.
  • ❌ Do not mix wet waste with dry waste.
  • 🚫 Do not throw plastics in the wet waste dustbin.
  • 💧 Avoid unnecessary water usage because water bills are paid by ourselves.
  • 👕 Wash clothes in full loads instead of washing only a few clothes separately, Please avoid washing only a few clothes separately like 3–4 shirts, pants, socks, or underwear at different times..
  • 🍽️ If you cook personal food, after eating clean your vessels, kitchen area, and sink.
⚠️ Special Cooking Rule

If someone does not know cooking, that person is still responsible for:
  • Deciding what to cook
  • Buying or arranging ingredients
  • Cutting vegetables
  • Preparing everything needed
  • Managing kitchen work
That person can take help from another roommate for actual cooking only.

The helper only stands and cooks. All remaining responsibilities belong to the assigned person.

This help system rotates weekly — if one person helps this week, another roommate should help next week.
🏠 Final Note

Everyone should cooperate to keep the room clean, maintain hygiene, save water, and reduce unnecessary expenses.
